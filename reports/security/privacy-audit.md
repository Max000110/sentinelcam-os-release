# Privacy & Surveillance Safeguards

## 1. Surveillance Controls
- **One-Touch Privacy Mode**: Disables AI inference and blurs video capture hardware pipeline.
- **Visual Foreground Notification**: Continuous ongoing notification displayed whenever camera or microphone is active, complying with Android privacy requirements.
- **Consent Enforcement**: Face intelligence requires verified user opt-in before storing any template.
- **Evidence Integrity**: Recordings can be locked against deletion, preventing tampering with critical security footage.
